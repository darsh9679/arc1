## Project-gallery visual references

Each of the seven project galleries now contains at least 12 images. The five projects with supplied photography show one project photo plus 11 Unsplash visual references. Dahisar and Vikhroli have 12 illustrative references each while approved project photography is unavailable. Every reference is labeled in the gallery and lightbox, and its caption links to the original Unsplash photo page. These images provide visual context only; they are not records of Trayaksh Architeqts work. The reference metadata and source pages are in `lib/project-references.generated.json`.

The reference photos were selected from Unsplash pages marked free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The site serves those references directly from the Unsplash image CDN, so an internet connection is required to load them.
